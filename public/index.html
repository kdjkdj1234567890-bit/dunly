Reminder

The model never owns the money.

1. AI reads, humans approve

The model turns messy text into lines. Nothing is sent until you review the draft. Missing details become questions, not guesses.

2. Prices are checked against your text

Each price must appear in what the client wrote. If it does not, the line is flagged and the send button stays locked until you confirm it.

3. Reminders cannot invent amounts

For collections, the invoice is re-read from PayPal. The only figure a reminder may contain is the amount due. Any other figure blocks the send, on the server.

4. Pasted text is data

Instructions hidden inside a client message are ignored. Try the third sample to see it.

What runs where

PayPal Invoicing API: create, send, list, remind, record payment, cancel. Gemini 2.5 Flash: draft and tone. Static page and serverless functions on Vercel. Source is open under MIT.

Limits of this demo

Sandbox only. Rate limited. One currency per invoice. No tax or discounts yet. Not financial or legal advice.
